About Purp

Purp is an AI-powered portfolio tracking tool delivered as a Google Chrome browser extension, developed and operated by André Soluções LTDA, a company incorporated under Brazilian law, registered under CNPJ 59.455.448/0001-42, headquartered in Sorocaba, São Paulo, Brazil.

Purp allows users to monitor investments across stocks and cryptocurrencies, receive AI-generated portfolio analysis, set price and performance alerts, import transaction history, and access tax planning tools. Our backend services run at purp-production.up.railway.app and our website is located at getpurpai.com.
