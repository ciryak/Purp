Features

Everything to invest smarter

One extension. Every market. Powered by Gemini AI to surface insights you'd otherwise miss.

🧩

Chrome Extension

Works seamlessly in your browser. Click the icon on any portfolio page and get instant AI-powered analysis without leaving the site.

🤖

Gemini AI Insights

Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash analyzes each asset and your full portfolio in plain English — no jargon, just actionable guidance.

📥

Broker CSV Import

Import trade history from Coinbase, Robinhood, Fidelity, Kraken, and Schwab. Your entire history syncs in under 30 seconds.

🔔

Smart Price Alerts

Set price-above, price-below, or percentage-change alerts for any asset. Get emailed the instant your threshold is hit.

📉

Tax Loss Harvesting

Our engine scans your portfolio for harvestable losses, calculates wash-sale safe dates, and estimates exact dollar savings for your bracket.

📊

Portfolio Analytics

Compare your performance against the S&P 500, Nasdaq, or Bitcoin across 1D to 2Y. FIFO, LIFO, and HIFO cost-basis methods included.

How it works

Up and running in 3 minutes

No open-banking API keys. No account linking. Just install, add your holdings, and let the AI go to work.

1

Install the extension

Add Purp to Chrome in one click. Create a free account with your email — no credit card required.

2

Add your assets

Type any ticker (BTC, AAPL, ETH) and enter your quantity and buy price — or import a full CSV from your broker in seconds.

3

Let AI optimize

Real-time prices, AI analysis, price alerts, and tax-saving recommendations. Your portfolio, finally working for you.
