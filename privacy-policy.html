Section 05

Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Portfolio data and account information are retained for the lifetime of your account.
  • Deleted accounts: Personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except where retention is legally required (e.g., billing records, typically 5 years under Brazilian fiscal law).
  • Crash logs (Sentry): Auto-deleted after 90 days.
  • Server access logs (IP): Deleted after 30 days.
  • Inactive Free accounts: Accounts with no login for more than 12 months may be deleted after 30 days' prior email notice.
Section 06

Security

We implement technical and organizational measures aligned with industry standards:

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we never store plaintext passwords.
  • Authentication tokens are signed JWTs with expiration periods.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher on all connections.
  • Data at rest is encrypted on Railway's infrastructure.
  • Broker API keys are stored using AES-256 encryption.
  • Payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe's PCI-DSS compliant systems.
⚠️
Security Notice
No system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access, contact us immediately at security@getpurpai.com. In case of a data breach, we will notify affected users and the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD) within the timeframes required by the LGPD.
Section 07

Extension Storage & Cookies

Chrome Extension Local Storage

The Purp extension uses the browser's local storage within the extension's isolated context to store:

  • Your authentication token (JWT) to keep you logged in.
  • Your email address for session restoration.
  • A pending verification flag if your email is unconfirmed.
  • Cached market data with a short TTL (60 seconds) to reduce API calls.

This data is stored only within Purp's isolated extension storage. It is not accessible to websites you visit or to any other extension. Purp does not inject scripts into web pages you browse.

Website Cookies

Our website at getpurpai.com uses only essential session cookies required for login functionality. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party analytics on our website.

No Cross-Site Tracking

Purp does not track your browsing activity across websites. The extension activates only within its own popup window and has no visibility into your browsing habits.

Section 08

Your Rights Under the LGPD

Under Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (Lei nº 13.709/2018), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

📋 Confirmation & Access (Art. 18, I–II)
Request confirmation that we process your data and a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
✏️ Correction (Art. 18, III)
Request correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated personal data. Most data can be edited directly within the extension.
🗑️ Anonymization, Blocking, or Deletion (Art. 18, IV)
Request deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in violation of the LGPD.
📦 Portability (Art. 18, V)
Request an export of your portfolio and transaction data in JSON or CSV format to transfer to another service.
🔔 Third-Party Info (Art. 18, VI)
Request information about third parties (public and private entities) with which we share your data.
🚫 Withdrawal of Consent (Art. 18, IX)
Withdraw consent for processing activities based on your consent (such as AI analysis) at any time.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Email privacy@getpurpai.com with the subject line "Privacy Request — [Right Type]" from the email address on your account. We will respond within 15 days as required by the LGPD. We may verify your identity before processing the request.

You also have the right to file a complaint with the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD) at gov.br/anpd.

Email Communications

  • Transactional (mandatory): Verification codes, price alert notifications, billing receipts, security notices. Required for the Service to function.
  • Marketing (optional): Product updates and announcements. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in any marketing email or by contacting us.
